👋👋👋 Hello, hello! 
<div style="text-align: justify;">

<p>I'm <strong>Hoa Nguyen</strong>, currently an ATER (Attaché Temporaire d'Enseignement et de Recherche) in Computer Science at <a href="https://esisar.grenoble-inp.fr/">Grenoble INP – Esisar</a>, affiliated with the <a href="https://lcis.grenoble-inp.fr/">LCIS laboratory</a> in Valence, France. I teach cryptography, algorithms and programming, and operating systems, and I continue my research in symmetric-key cryptanalysis.</p>

<p>Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher (2025–2026) in the <a href="https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/research/labs/information-processing-ltci/teams/cybersecurity-cryptography">C2 (Cybersecurity and Cryptography) team</a> at <a href="https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home">Télécom Paris</a>, <a href="https://www.ip-paris.fr/en">Institut Polytechnique de Paris</a>, working with <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/qingjuwang">Qingju Wang</a> on the design and evaluation of lightweight cryptographic primitives for constrained environments and Multi-Party Computation (MPC).</p>

<p>I completed my PhD (defended in February 2025) in the <a href="https://team.inria.fr/capsule/">CAPSULE team</a> at <a href="https://www.irisa.fr/en">IRISA</a>, France, under the supervision of <a href="https://people.irisa.fr/Pierre-Alain.Fouque/">Pierre-Alain Fouque</a> and <a href="https://people.irisa.fr/Patrick.Derbez/">Patrick Derbez</a>. My doctoral research explored the art of cryptanalysis of symmetric primitives, with a primary focus on developing automated tools using MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Programming) models and optimization techniques. You can find my thesis <a href="https://theses.hal.science/tel-05369410">here</a>.</p>

<p>In the lab, I solve for <var>X</var>; in the real world, I’m usually just looking for the next "Spot <var>X</var>" on a map. Whether I'm optimizing a lightweight primitive or a lightweight backpack, I’m always searching for the most efficient route to a new culture.</p>

</div>
