Academic year 2026–2027, as ATER and module lead. Taught in English.

The course covers the fundamentals of modern cryptography: classical ciphers and attack models, symmetric primitives, modes of operation, hash functions and MACs, public-key cryptography, protocols, and post-quantum cryptography. It is organised in lectures (CM), pen-and-paper tutorials (TD) and programming labs in Python (TP).
