We develop an automated framework that searches for and optimizes differential meet-in-the-middle attacks on bit-oriented block ciphers, tailored to AndRX constructions. A unified bitwise CP model finds optimal differential characteristics and builds the attack, with dedicated key-recovery optimizations (equivalent subkeys, selective key guessing, parallel partitioning). Applied to the full SIMON and Simeck families, it extends the round coverage of previous meet-in-the-middle and Demirci–Selçuk-type attacks.
